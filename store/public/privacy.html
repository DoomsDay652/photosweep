Photo Sweep privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 3, 2026.

Dreamy Game Studios (D.G.S.) provides Photo Sweep (Android package com.dominic.photosweep). This policy covers the app, our account deletion website and support communications. Contact us about privacy at dreamygamestudios.support@gmail.com.

Photos, permissions and device information

Photo Sweep accesses only the photos permitted through Android. It reads photo content and media information such as photo identifiers, dates and file sizes to show photos by month, find exact duplicates, and carry out your keep, Trash and restore choices. Photo content and this media information are processed on your device; Photo Sweep does not upload them to Dreamy Game Studios or Firebase.

Review progress, preferences, theme settings and Trash recovery records are stored locally. Gravity or accelerometer readings animate tilt effects on your device; we do not upload or retain a history of these readings. We do not request access to your contacts, microphone or precise location.

Photo access is requested when you choose to grant it. You can decline, permit selected photos on compatible Android versions, or grant broader photo access. Optional Android Manage media access reduces confirmation prompts and permits automatic removal of expired Trash entries. Android controls media access, Trash retention and final deletion; Photo Sweep cannot guarantee recovery after permanent deletion. Change or revoke permissions in Android app settings.

Optional accounts and Firebase

You can use Photo Sweep as a guest. If you sign in with Google, Google and Firebase Authentication process your email address, name, profile image URL, Google sign-in identifiers and authentication tokens to create and authenticate your Photo Sweep account. We request basic sign-in information, not access to your Gmail, Google Drive or cloud photos. Existing email accounts remain available; Firebase processes their email address, username (display name) and password credentials. For both sign-in methods, Firebase also processes technical information, including IP addresses and user-agent information, to maintain sessions and prevent abuse. Verification and password reset emails apply to email/password accounts. Firebase handles password credentials; Photo Sweep does not save your plaintext password. The Firebase SDK maintains account and session information on your device so you can stay signed in.

You can edit your username in the app. Usernames are display names, are not unique and are not sign-in credentials; sign in with Google or, for an email/password account, your email address and password. You can link Google sign-in to an existing authenticated email account. We do not sync photos or review progress to your account. See Firebase privacy and security information for Google's processing practices.

Support website and email

Our public privacy and deletion pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser information and request times to deliver and protect its services under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add advertising cookies or analytics scripts to these pages.

If you use the deletion form, the email and password you enter are sent directly over HTTPS to Firebase to verify account ownership and delete the account. Our page does not store these credentials in browser storage. Your browser or password manager may save credentials according to your settings.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Gmail processes these communications under Google's Privacy Policy. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal photos.

Sharing and security

We use Google Firebase for authentication, GitHub for website hosting and Gmail for support. These providers process the data described above. We do not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers in this release. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to respond to a valid legal request.

Account and website requests use HTTPS encryption in transit. Local app records are stored in Android's app storage, and Android backup is disabled for this app. Authentication secrets are handled by Firebase; the deletion page keeps its temporary authentication token in memory for the request rather than persisting it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Firebase retains account information while your account exists. To permanently delete it, use Options > Account & support > Delete account in the app or our account deletion page. Ownership verification is required. In the app, Google accounts are verified through Google sign-in and email accounts through their password. Deleting your Photo Sweep account does not delete your Google account. The website offers an email/password deletion form and password reset. Google sign-in users can request deletion without reinstalling the app by emailing support from the email address linked to their Photo Sweep account; we verify ownership before deleting it. Do not send passwords. Contact support if you cannot complete deletion. Successful deletion removes the active Firebase account, including its email, display name and account identifiers; it is not merely disabled.

According to Firebase's published retention information, logged IP addresses are kept for a few weeks. After account deletion is initiated, other authentication information is removed from Firebase's live and backup systems within 180 days. This provider retention is separate from removal of your active account.

Local progress and settings remain until you reset them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Account deletion does not delete your device photos, Android Trash or local guest progress. The local reset clears review and customization data without deleting your Firebase account or photos. Android manages retention of trashed photos separately.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us. If any information must be retained for a legal or security reason, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period when responding, unless prohibited by law. Hosting and email providers manage their own service logs and backups under their policies.

You may sign out, use guest mode, revoke photo permissions, edit your display name or delete your account. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information, or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact the privacy email above to make a request. We may need to verify your identity; do not send your password. Firebase and our other providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States.

Advertising, purchases and policy updates

This release has no active advertising or checkout. Photo Sweep does not initiate payments in this release. If you choose Restore purchases, the Google Play billing library queries the Play account currently used by the Play Store for owned in-app products. Google Play returns product identifiers, purchase status, acknowledgement status and purchase tokens; Photo Sweep uses this information only to check ownership and restore completed, acknowledged ad-removal purchases. The restored ad-removal status is stored on your device. We do not upload purchase tokens to Firebase or receive your payment-card details. Purchase ownership belongs to your Google Play account and is separate from Photo Sweep sign-in. Consumed support purchases cannot be restored. Before advertising or purchases are enabled, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety disclosures to explain the providers, data handling and relevant choices.

We will publish revisions at this URL with an updated date and provide additional notice or obtain consent when required for material changes. This policy describes the current release; it does not grant permission for undisclosed future data collection.
