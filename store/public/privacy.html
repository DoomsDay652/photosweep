Photos and local information

Photo Sweep processes photos you permit on your Android device to review them, find exact duplicates and move or restore them through Android Trash. It does not upload photos. Review progress, preferences and Trash recovery records are stored locally. Android controls media access and final Trash removal. We request photo access when you choose to grant it. Selected photo access is supported on compatible Android versions. Manage media access is optional and allows fewer Android confirmation prompts and automatic removal of expired Trash entries.

Optional accounts

When accounts are enabled, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, authentication identifiers, password credentials and service data such as IP addresses needed to authenticate and protect accounts. Passwords are handled by Firebase; Photo Sweep does not save your plaintext password. We do not sync photos or progress to an account. You may use Photo Sweep as a guest.

Deletion and retention

You can permanently delete your authentication account in the app or through our account deletion page. This removes the Firebase authentication record. Firebase may retain security/service logs according to its own retention practices. Photos, Android Trash, local guest progress and Play purchases remain separate. Reset local progress clears review preferences; uninstalling removes local app data. You can revoke permissions in Android app settings.

Advertising and purchases

This release has no active advertising or checkout. Before these services are enabled, this policy and the Play Data safety disclosures will be updated to describe their data handling.
